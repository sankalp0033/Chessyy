Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this academy for? +
BY SANKALP is designed for chess players of all levels — from complete beginners learning the fundamentals to advanced tournament players seeking master-level insights. Our structured curriculum adapts to your current rating and goals.
How does the Lifetime plan work? +
The Lifetime plan requires a single one-time payment of ₹45,499. After that, you receive unlimited, permanent access to all current features and all future updates, new courses, tools, and premium content — forever. No recurring charges, ever.
Can beginners join? +
Absolutely! Our Basic plan is specifically crafted for beginners. It includes foundational courses, weekly assignments, puzzle practice, and a supportive community. We take you from understanding the rules to playing confidently.
Will new content be added regularly? +
Yes. We continuously expand our Video Library, Articles, Puzzle collections, and course materials. Premium and Lifetime members receive all new content and feature updates automatically at no additional cost.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. Monthly and yearly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period. The Lifetime plan is a one-time purchase and does not require cancellation.
